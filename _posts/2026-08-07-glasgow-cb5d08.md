## The Shape of Glasgow

Few ideas are invoked as often, and examined as rarely, as glasgow. The usual shorthand, that glasgow is the most populous city in Scotland, located on the banks of the River Clyde in west central Scotland, is accurate enough, yet it leaves the more interesting question untouched: why does the concept hold together at all?



To call glasgow well understood would be premature; to call it mysterious would be lazy. The truth sits in between, a concept with real structure, real consequences, and real limits, and it is in mapping those limits that understanding begins.
