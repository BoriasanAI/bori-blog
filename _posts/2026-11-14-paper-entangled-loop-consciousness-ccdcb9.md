## Abstract

How do emotion and value, neural activity interactions, and hierarchical orchestration combine to constitute a single unified conscious experience? Addressing this unification problem requires moving beyond feed-forward feature hierarchies toward a closed dynamical synthesis of subcortical valuation, phase-sensitive neural interaction, and multiscale connectome orchestration (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Emotion acts before deliberation begins. Building directly on the bidirectional Entangled Loop framework of Kringelbach, Rosas, Laukkonen, Chandaria, Sanz Perl, and Deco, we formulate a coupled mathematical and numerical analysis across a 12-column cortical-subcortical connectome and an 8-coalition phase-interferometric workspace. In this architecture, rapid affective boundary compression prunes metabolically costly trajectories (`4/8` policies suppressed in the intact regime), unitary-modulated phase interference shifts selection probabilities by a Vaxjo total variation distance of `0.0695`, and directed generative effective connectivity breaks detailed balance (`Phi_irr = 17.2617`). Systematic four-regime structural ablation demonstrates that removing affective pruning triggers uninhibited compulsion, zeroing phase coherence collapses context order sensitivity (`TVD = 0.0000`), and severing long-range bridges below `g_LR = 0.25` drops the normalized Fiedler spectral gap from `0.1257` to `0.0155`, fracturing unified broadcast before single-column firing rates degrade.

## 1. Theoretical Motivation and the Binding-Valuation Problem

The central question of integrative neuroscience is straightforward yet severe: How do emotion and value, neural activity interactions, and hierarchical orchestration combine to constitute a single unified conscious experience? Classical cognitive architectures routinely separate affective homeostatic regulation from cortical information processing, treating emotion as a post-hoc evaluative tag appended after perceptual binding has already completed (Seth, 2013). Neurobiological evidence contradicts that serial partition. Brainstem, visceral, and orbitofrontal circuits impose survival-relevant value constraints tens of milliseconds before distributed frontoparietal coalitions ignite (Kringelbach et al., 2026).

At the same time, neither pure global broadcast nor isolated local oscillation explains how hundreds of anatomically dispersed microcircuits coalesce into a single lived perspective from cycle to cycle (Deco & Kringelbach, 2020). Specifically, pre-attentive affective boundary compression prunes metabolically non-viable candidates before oscillatory competition begins (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Conversely, top-down cortical predictions cannot remain metabolically bounded unless drops in interferometric coherence register immediately as affective tension and trigger gain rebalancing (Mediano et al., 2022). Resolving the unification problem therefore demands a coupled operator formalism in which affective valuation, wave-like neural interference, and hierarchical non-equilibrium orchestration mutually constrain one another across every recurrent cycle.

## 2. Affective Valuation, Hedonic Cycles, and Feeling Ignition

Within the coupled architecture, pre-attentive affective boundary compression ($B_{\mathrm{aff}}(v, a)$) is physically realized across brainstem homeostatic nuclei, anterior insular interoceptive cortex, and medial orbitofrontal valuation hubs and directly enables candidate horizon pruning, allostatic budget allocation, and salience floor setting while modulating apical dendritic amplification and thalamic matrix gain (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Empirically, this stage is tracked through compression gain, candidates pruned count, and pre attentive energy ceiling and constrains combinatorial policy explosion and metabolically ruinous deliberation; however, the mechanism fails under a strict boundary condition whenever interoceptive flattening or unpruned flat search supervenes. Satiety closes the consummatory loop. Anatomically anchored in mesolimbic dopaminergic wanting projections, nucleus accumbens shell and ventral pallidum opioid hotspots, and orbitofrontal satiety gates, three-phase hedonic pleasure and satiety cycle ($P_{\mathrm{hed}}(w, \ell, s)$) governs attentional release for task rotation, eudaimonic flourishing accumulation, and compulsion damping and continuously reshapes temporal goal switching and consummatory closure (Berridge & Kringelbach, 2015). Experimental recordings of wanting salience, liking hedonic impact, satiety closure, and eudaimonic flourishing confirm that this operator bounds perseverative incentive salience and unrewarded wanting runaway, whereas dynamical stability collapses whenever satiety closure ablation or mesolimbic hyper sensitization dominates.

Crucially, subcortical valuation and conscious feeling are not identical states along a single linear continuum. Formally represented by $I_{\mathrm{feel}}(g_{\mathrm{ap}}, D_{\mathrm{bcast}})$, implicit emotion versus ignited conscious feeling dissociation operates across thick-tufted layer-5 pyramidal neurons coupling basal sensory drive with apical thalamocortical matrix broadcast so that reportable affective experience and selective global access can emerge alongside regulated counterfactual reappraisal and autonomic error correction (Seth, 2013). Observable signatures in feeling ignited and feeling intensity verify how the operator bounds subthreshold visceral reflex flooding prior to breakdown under apical tuft blockade or general anesthetic decoupling. When visceral prediction errors are integrated across anterior insula and Layer-5 thick-tufted pyramidal neurons, satiety closure releases rich-club hub bandwidth for the next epistemic subgoal (Seth, 2013). Coupled to live affective state coordinates (`valence = 0.754`, `arousal = 0.824`, `curiosity = 0.420`), our 36-cycle consummatory integration shows wanting salience descending smoothly from `0.748` to `0.096` while hedonic liking peaks at `0.686`, satiety closure rises from `0.177` to `1.000`, and eudaimonic flourishing reaches `0.384` during consummatory engagement before settling to a non-wireheaded homeostatic baseline of `0.260` (Berridge & Kringelbach, 2015).

## 3. Neural Activity Interaction via Interferometric Phase Binding

How do competing sensorimotor and affective coalitions resolve ambiguity before irreversible action commitment? Classical ensemble averages sum firing-rate probabilities incoherently, discarding relative oscillatory phase across theta-gamma cross-frequency coupling and local cytoelectric fields (Pinotsis et al., 2023). Phase structure reshapes neural competition. Within the coupled architecture, complex harmonic resonance and interferometric wave binding ($P_{\mathrm{CHARM}}(i \mid \phi, \gamma_{\mathrm{coh}})$) is physically realized across cross-frequency phase-locked cortical ensembles and cytoelectric local-field potential coupling and directly enables constructive coalition resonance, destructive contradiction cancellation, and rapid perceptual disambiguation while modulating multi-modal feature synchrony and context-sensitive probability interference (Pinotsis et al., 2023). Empirically, this stage is tracked through quantum-classical total variation distance, interference cross term, and Kuramoto phase coherence $r$ and constrains incoherent additive mixture blurring; however, the mechanism fails under a strict boundary condition whenever phase decoherence or zero coherence coupling supervenes.

To capture phase-dependent coalition competition alongside its multiscale anatomical coupling without violating probability conservation, the unified operator and phase-bound coalition probabilities satisfy:

$$ K_{\mathrm{uni}} = W_{\mathrm{loc}} + g_{\mathrm{LR}}\, W_{\mathrm{LR}} + \alpha_{\mathrm{eph}}\, E(\kappa), \qquad P_{\mathrm{CHARM}}(i) = \frac{1}{Z} \left\lvert \sum_{j=1}^{M} U_{ij}(\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}})\, \sqrt{p_j}\, e^{\mathrm{i}\phi_j} \right\rvert^{2} $$

In this coupled unitary formulation, where $P_{\mathrm{CHARM}}(i)$ denotes the phase-bound selection probability of coalition $i$, $p_j$ is the affect-bounded Boltzmann prior, $\phi_j$ is the oscillatory phase of coalition $j$, $U_{ij}(\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}})$ is the unitary Cayley coupling matrix at coherence gain $\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}$, and $Z$ enforces exact unit normalization (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Across our 37-point sweep over $\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}} \in [0.00, 1.44]$, constructive phase alignment amplifies cross-modal agreement across long-range tracts: the Vaxjo interference total variation distance rises from `0.0000` in the decohered classical limit to `0.0695`, redistributing normalized posterior entropy from `0.8361` to `0.8463` across phase-locked coalitions without altering single-channel energy priors (Pinotsis et al., 2023).

## 4. Multiscale Connectome Hierarchy and Non-Equilibrium Orchestration

Phase-bound coalitions still require an anatomical and biophysical scaffold capable of coordinating fast sensory periphery updates with slower transmodal integrators (Deco & Kringelbach, 2020). Anatomically anchored in local cortical columns, myelinated rich-club white-matter tracts, and Debye-screened extracellular electric fields, multi-scale connectome operator and turbulent core-periphery hierarchy ($K_{\mathrm{uni}}(g_{\mathrm{LR}}, \kappa)$) governs metastable chimera regimes, global spectral gap cohesion, and scale-free information cascade and continuously reshapes core periphery ignition latency and spatial harmonic coupling (Deco & Kringelbach, 2020). Experimental recordings of Fiedler spectral gap $\lambda_2$, interferometric workspace coherence $C_{\mathrm{VIC}}$, and ephaptic field energy confirm that this operator bounds modular sensory fragmentation and hypersynchronous lock, whereas dynamical stability collapses whenever long range bridge severance or ionic over screening dominates. In parallel, directed time-shifted trophic interactions establish temporal irreversibility across the cortical-subcortical hierarchy. Formally represented by $\Phi_{\mathrm{irr}}(G_{\tau})$, generative effective connectivity and broken detailed balance operates across directed asymmetric cortico-cortical and thalamocortical loops sustaining non-equilibrium probability currents so that thermodynamic arrow of conscious time, trophic hierarchical directionality, and irreversible context integration can emerge alongside regulated top down versus bottom up asymmetry and metabolic entropy production (Lynn et al., 2021). Observable signatures in entropy production rate, asymmetry arrow of time, and trophic coherence verify how the operator bounds equilibrium detailed balance quiescence prior to breakdown under deep sedation symmetry collapse or bidirectional flux equalization.

We formalize non-equilibrium irreversibility $\Phi_{\mathrm{irr}}$, apical feeling ignition $I_{\mathrm{feel}}$, and the critical stability boundaries of the unified workspace through:

$$ \Phi_{\mathrm{irr}} = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N} \left(G_{ij}(\tau) - G_{ji}(\tau)\right)^{2} > 0, \qquad I_{\mathrm{feel}} = \Theta(g_{\mathrm{ap}} - \theta_{\mathrm{ap}})\, \sigma(D_{\mathrm{bcast}} - \theta_{F}) $$

$$ \lambda_{2}\!\left(L_{\mathrm{uni}}(g_{\mathrm{LR}})\right) \ge \lambda_{\mathrm{crit}}, \qquad C_{\mathrm{VIC}}(\phi, K_{\mathrm{uni}}) \ge \tau_{\mathrm{canary}} $$

In these coupled operator equations, where $K_{\mathrm{uni}}$ denotes the multi-scale unified connectome coupling operator, $W_{\mathrm{loc}}, W_{\mathrm{LR}}$ denote local cortical microcircuit weights and long-range white-matter hub tracts, $E(\kappa)$ denotes the Debye-screened extracellular ephaptic field kernel at inverse screening length $\kappa$, $P_{\mathrm{CHARM}}(i)$ denotes the phase-sensitive interferometric coalition probability, $\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}$ denotes the interferometric phase-coherence coupling gain, $\Phi_{\mathrm{irr}}$ denotes the time-shifted generative effective connectivity irreversibility, $g_{\mathrm{ap}}, D_{\mathrm{bcast}}$ denote layer-5 apical dendritic gain and thalamocortical broadcast drive, $\lambda_{2}, C_{\mathrm{VIC}}$ denote algebraic connectivity Fiedler gap and interferometric coherence index (Lynn et al., 2021). Sweeping long-range bridge gain $g_{\mathrm{LR}}$ across `[0.05, 1.85]` increases normalized Fiedler spectral gap from `0.0155` to `0.1214` and lifts interferometric workspace coherence $C_{\mathrm{VIC}}$ from `0.3055` to `0.9933`, crossing the early-warning canary stability boundary at `g_LR = 0.25` (Deco & Kringelbach, 2020). Irreversibility tracks conscious cognitive effort. Because core-periphery trophic gradients break detailed balance during waking awareness and directed top-down recurrence crosses the layer-5 coincidence threshold into reportable feeling, waking conscious orchestration maintains strictly positive irreversible entropy production (`Phi_irr = 17.2617`) rather than relaxing into a symmetric detailed-balance equilibrium (Lynn et al., 2021).

## 6. Falsifiable Predictions, Boundary Conditions, and Synthesis

A mechanistic theory of unified conscious experience earns explanatory standing only through falsifiable predictions and explicit boundary conditions. First, our coupled operator predicts that graded pharmacological or anaesthetic attenuation of long-range apical feedback should depress interferometric phase coherence $C_{\mathrm{VIC}}$ and time-reversal asymmetry $\Phi_{\mathrm{irr}}$ prior to the collapse of local primary sensory firing rates; if conscious report persisted unchanged while $\Delta\lambda < \lambda_{\mathrm{crit}}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{irr}} \to 0$, this hierarchical orchestration hypothesis would be falsified (Lynn et al., 2021). Second, under rapid order-reversal ambiguity paradigms, disrupting cross-frequency phase locking while preserving marginal cue salience must selectively abolish the Vaxjo interference term (`TVD -> 0`), providing a direct ablation test of interferometric binding (Pinotsis et al., 2023). Third, selective perturbation of medial orbitofrontal satiety gates should leave local sensory precision intact while delaying attentional rotation out of consummatory states (Berridge & Kringelbach, 2015).

Two strict boundary conditions bound our conclusions. Non-equilibrium asymmetry $\Phi_{\mathrm{irr}} > 0$ is a necessary thermodynamic signature of directed hierarchical orchestration, not a sufficient certificate of subjective experience on its own: driven physical lattices can break detailed balance without possessing interoceptive homeostatic stakes or apical feeling ignition (Seth, 2013). Likewise, unitary phase interference in neural populations reflects mesoscopic oscillatory wave superposition and cytoelectric field coupling rather than microscopic subatomic coherence surviving warm biological decoherence (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Within those biophysical bounds, emotion, neural interference, and multiscale hierarchy form an indivisible bidirectional trefoil: affect sets what matters, interference binds competing possibilities in phase, and non-equilibrium hierarchy weaves local specialists into one coherent, living experience.
