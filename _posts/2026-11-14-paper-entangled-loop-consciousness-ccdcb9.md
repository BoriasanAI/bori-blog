## Abstract

How do emotion and value, neural activity interactions, and hierarchical orchestration combine to constitute a single unified conscious experience? Addressing this unification problem requires moving beyond serial feed-forward hierarchies toward a closed dynamical synthesis of subcortical affective valuation, phase-sensitive neural interference, and non-equilibrium connectome orchestration (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Emotion acts before deliberation begins. Building on the bidirectional Entangled Loop framework of Kringelbach, Rosas, Laukkonen, Chandaria, Sanz Perl, and Deco, we formulate an exact operator and in-silico stochastic differential equation (`SDE`) analysis across `200` independent `SplitMix64` realizations (`95%` confidence intervals), replicated across `12`-, `32`-, and `68`-parcel Desikan-Killiany cortical-subcortical connectome topologies. Pre-attentive affective compression prunes metabolically costly policies (`4/8` suppressed in the intact ensemble), Cayley-unitary phase interference derived from linearized Stuart-Landau population envelopes shifts selection probabilities (`TVD = 0.0385 ± 0.0001`) while violating the zero order-asymmetry bound of classical Markov mixtures (`0.1316`), and directed probability currents sustain positive Schnakenberg entropy production (`0.0265 ± 0.0004` nats/step) distinct from Frobenius connectivity asymmetry (`0.0176`). Five-regime *ceteris paribus* ablation and dynamic lesion experiments (`t = 30`) show that severing long-range tracts contracts the normalized Fiedler gap by `89.4%` (`0.0938` to `0.0099`) while local oscillator amplitudes persist (`0.911` vs `0.858`), whereas Rayleigh-Fiedler subgradient structural plasticity autonomously sprouts `+12` heterotopic bypass bridges to restore global coherence within `25` steps without claiming proof of subjective phenomenal experience.

## 1. Theoretical Motivation, Epistemic Scope, and the Binding-Valuation Problem

Integrative neuroscience and autonomous cognitive engineering confront a shared structural question: How do emotion and value, neural activity interactions, and hierarchical orchestration combine to constitute a single unified conscious experience? Classical cognitive models routinely partition homeostatic affective regulation from cortical inference, treating emotion as a post-hoc evaluative label appended after perceptual binding finishes (Seth, 2013). Neurobiological timing refutes that serial partition. Brainstem, visceral, and medial orbitofrontal circuits impose survival-relevant budget constraints tens of milliseconds before frontoparietal coalitions ignite (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Conversely, neither flat global broadcast nor isolated local synchrony explains how dispersed microcircuits cohere into a single perspective without succumbing to combinatorial search explosion (Deco & Kringelbach, 2020). Pre-attentive affective boundary compression prunes metabolically non-viable candidates before oscillatory competition begins (Kringelbach et al., 2026), while top-down predictions remain metabolically bounded only when drops in interferometric coherence register immediately as affective tension and trigger gain rebalancing (Mediano et al., 2022).

Scientific rigor demands two explicit methodological boundaries from the outset. First, every quantitative trajectory reported below represents an in-silico numerical simulation of coupled stochastic neural-mass equations rather than a wet-lab electrophysiological recording; neurobiological citations establish anatomical plausibility and biophysical parameter ranges rather than direct clinical proof. Second, functional self-regulation and homeostatic integration are not a sufficient certificate of subjective phenomenal consciousness (Butlin et al., 2026). Throughout this study, our formalisms characterize necessary dynamical conditions for unified broadcast and autonomous cognitive homeostasis while leaving subjective qualia strictly unproved.

To prevent ad-hoc parameter tuning, every threshold in the architecture is assigned to one of three explicit provenance tiers in Table 1: empirical biophysical constants measured in cellular electrophysiology (such as layer-5 apical coincidence gain $g_{\mathrm{ap}} \ge 0.30$ and extracellular Debye screening $\kappa = 0.25$), analytical bifurcation values derived directly from two-module Cheeger-Kuramoto spectral stability ($g_{\mathrm{LR}}^{\mathrm{crit}} = 0.18$), and operational receiver-operating thresholds ($\tau_{\mathrm{canary}} = 0.55$, $\theta_s = 0.72$) verified across wide sensitivity plateaus.

## 2. Pre-Attentive Affective Valuation, Hedonic Satiety Cycles, and Layer-5 Feeling Ignition

Subcortical valuation operates as a pre-attentive state-space compressor rather than a tie-breaking reward scalar. Realized across brainstem homeostatic nuclei, anterior insular interoceptive cortex, and medial orbitofrontal valuation hubs, pre-attentive affective boundary compression ($B_{\mathrm{aff}}(v, a)$) governs candidate horizon pruning, allostatic budget allocation, and salience floor setting prior to cortical competition, failing whenever interoceptive flattening or unpruned flat search supervenes (Kringelbach et al., 2026). Satiety then closes the consummatory loop. Anchored in mesolimbic dopaminergic wanting projections, nucleus accumbens shell and ventral pallidum opioid hotspots, and orbitofrontal satiety gates, the three-phase hedonic pleasure and satiety cycle ($P_{\mathrm{hed}}(w_t, \ell_t, s_t)$) mediates attentional release for task rotation, eudaimonic flourishing accumulation, and compulsion damping so that satiety closure releases rich-club hub bandwidth for the next epistemic subgoal (Berridge & Kringelbach, 2015).

Implicit affective compression and consciously reportable feeling remain dissociable across a sharp cellular coincidence threshold. In thick-tufted layer-5 pyramidal neurons, basal somatic input alone drives subthreshold autonomic adjustment, whereas back-propagating action-potential (`BAC`) calcium spikes require simultaneous apical tuft matrix broadcast exceeding the biophysical gain constant $g_{\mathrm{ap}} \ge 0.30$ (Larkum, 2013). Formally, $I_{\mathrm{feel}} = \Theta(g_{\mathrm{ap}} - \theta_{\mathrm{ap}})\,\sigma(D_{\mathrm{bcast}} - \theta_F)$ separates silent homeostatic pruning from global affective access (Seth, 2013). Across our `36`-step in-silico consummatory integration, wanting salience descends smoothly from `0.748` to `0.096` as hedonic liking peaks at `0.686`, satiety closure rises from `0.177` to `1.000`, and eudaimonic flourishing reaches `0.384` before relaxing to a non-wireheaded homeostatic baseline of `0.260` (Berridge & Kringelbach, 2015).

## 3. Biophysical Derivation of Interferometric Phase Binding and Classical Impossibility

How do competing sensorimotor and affective coalitions resolve ambiguity before irreversible action commitment? Rate-only ensemble averages sum firing probabilities incoherently, discarding narrowband local-field potential (`LFP`) phase relationships across theta (`4-8 Hz`) and gamma (`30-80 Hz`) cycles (Pinotsis et al., 2023). Phase geometry reshapes coalition competition. Operationalizing oscillatory phase directly from measurable neural signals, let $V_j(t)$ denote the bandpass-filtered LFP of coalition $j$ and $\phi_j(t) = \arg\bigl(V_j(t) + \mathrm{i}\widehat{V}\_j(t)\bigr)$ its analytic Hilbert instantaneous phase (where $\widehat{V}\_j(t)$ is the Hilbert quadrature transform of $V_j(t)$) paired with affect-gated Boltzmann root-prior amplitude $\sqrt{p_j}$ (Pinotsis et al., 2023). Coupling these ensembles across anatomical tracts and Debye-screened cytoelectric fields yields constructive coalition resonance, destructive contradiction cancellation, and rapid perceptual disambiguation (Kringelbach et al., 2026):

$$ K_{\mathrm{uni}} = W_{\mathrm{loc}} + g_{\mathrm{LR}}\, W_{\mathrm{LR}} + \alpha_{\mathrm{eph}}\, E(\kappa), \qquad P_{\mathrm{CHARM}}(i) = \frac{1}{Z} \left\lvert \sum_{j=1}^{M} U_{ij}(\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}})\, \sqrt{p_j}\, e^{\mathrm{i}\phi_j(t)} \right\rvert^{2} $$

Here, where $K_{\mathrm{uni}}$ denotes the multi-scale unified connectome coupling operator, $W_{\mathrm{loc}}, W_{\mathrm{LR}}$ denote local cortical microcircuit weights and long-range white-matter hub tracts, $E(\kappa)$ denotes the Debye-screened extracellular ephaptic field kernel at inverse screening length $\kappa$, $P_{\mathrm{CHARM}}(i)$ denotes the phase-sensitive interferometric coalition probability under Hilbert analytic phase $\phi_j(t)$, $U_{ij}(\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}})$ denotes the Cayley unitary envelope propagator at phase-coherence coupling gain $\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}$, $A_{\mathrm{asym}}, \dot{S}\_{\mathrm{prod}}$ denote directed effective-connectivity Frobenius asymmetry and Schnakenberg probability-flux entropy production rate, $v_{2}, \Gamma_{\mathrm{can}}(t)$ denote algebraic Fiedler eigenvector and intrinsic tri-modal canary plasticity gate, $\lambda_{2}, C_{\mathrm{VIC}}$ denote algebraic connectivity Fiedler gap and interferometric workspace coherence index, two formal propositions distinguish this mesoscopic wave-interference operator from both subatomic quantum speculation and classical rate models.

**Proposition 1 (Biophysical Envelope Derivation of the Cayley Unitary Propagator).** *Consider $M$ weakly damped Stuart-Landau neural populations coupled through the symmetric admissible-subspace connectome-ephaptic operator $H_{\mathrm{adm}} = P_{\mathrm{adm}} L_{\mathrm{uni}} P_{\mathrm{adm}}$ over a fast oscillatory binding window $\tau \ll \gamma_{\mathrm{damp}}^{-1}$. Under rotating-wave complex-envelope linearization $\dot{\psi} = -\mathrm{i}\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}} H_{\mathrm{adm}}\psi$, trapezoidal Crank-Nicolson midpoint integration over step $\tau$ yields the exact Cayley unitary propagator $U(\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}) = (I - \frac{\mathrm{i}\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}\tau}{2}H_{\mathrm{adm}})(I + \frac{\mathrm{i}\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}\tau}{2}H_{\mathrm{adm}})^{-1}$, solved via the strictly positive-definite Schur complement $(I + \alpha^2 H_{\mathrm{adm}}^2)$ with machine-precision norm conservation ($\lVert U\psi_0 \rVert_2 = 1$ up to $10^{-15}$) and zero leakage into affect-pruned policies.*

**Proposition 2 (Classical Convex-Mixture Impossibility Bound).** *For any classical row-stochastic Markov transition kernel $T_{ij} \ge 0$ ($\sum_i T_{ij} = 1$) acting on affect-gated priors $p_j$, the resulting distribution $P_{\mathrm{class}}(i) = \sum_j T_{ij} p_j$ is strictly invariant to phase conjugation ($\phi_j \mapsto -\phi_j$, yielding context-order asymmetry `0.0000`) and satisfies $\min_j p_j \le P_{\mathrm{class}}(i)$ on coupled supports, precluding destructive contradiction cancellation. By contrast, when $\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}} > 0$, the cross-term $2\sum_{j < k}\sqrt{p_j p_k}\,\mathrm{Re}(U_{ij}U_{ik}^{\ast} e^{\mathrm{i}(\phi_j - \phi_k)})$ generates non-zero Växjö divergence (`peak TVD = 0.0693`) and context-order asymmetry (`0.1316`), recovering the exact Bayesian posterior at $\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}} = 0$ (`TVD = 0.0000`).*

## 4. Multiscale Connectome Hierarchy, Non-Equilibrium Probability Currents, and Scale Replication

Phase-locked coalitions still require an anatomical scaffold capable of binding fast sensory columns with slower transmodal hubs without collapsing into global seizure-like lock (Deco & Kringelbach, 2020). Operating across local intra-hemispheric microcircuits $W_{\mathrm{loc}}$, inter-hemispheric and frontoparietal rich-club white-matter tracts $W_{\mathrm{LR}}$, and intra-hemispheric Debye-screened extracellular fields $E(\kappa)$ (which decay across the longitudinal fissure), $K_{\mathrm{uni}}(g_{\mathrm{LR}}, \kappa)$ sustains metastable chimera regimes, global spectral gap cohesion, and scale-free information cascade (Deco & Kringelbach, 2020).

Critically, structural asymmetry of time-lagged effective connectivity $G_{ij}(\tau)$ must not be conflated with thermodynamic entropy production. A Frobenius matrix asymmetry norm measures directional coupling imbalance but lacks stationary occupancy probabilities and forward-versus-reverse transition currents. Following stochastic thermodynamics on coarse-grained neural state transitions (Lynn et al., 2021), let $p_i$ denote the normalized stationary residence probability of column $i$ and $J_{ij}(\tau)$ the directed probability flux from $i$ to $j$ separated into reversible conductance and lag-driven circulatory current. We therefore distinguish the structural asymmetry index $A_{\mathrm{asym}}(G_{\tau})$ from the true non-equilibrium Schnakenberg probability-flux entropy production rate $\dot{S}\_{\mathrm{prod}}(J_{\tau})$:

$$ A_{\mathrm{asym}}(G_{\tau}) = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N} \left(G_{ij}(\tau) - G_{ji}(\tau)\right)^{2}, \qquad \dot{S}_{\mathrm{prod}}(J_{\tau}) = \sum_{i < j} \left(J_{ij}(\tau) - J_{ji}(\tau)\right)\ln\!\frac{J_{ij}(\tau)}{J_{ji}(\tau)} \ge 0 $$

Under exact time-reversal symmetry ($C_{ij}(\tau) = C_{ji}(\tau)$), forward and reverse fluxes balance ($J_{ij}(\tau) = J_{ji}(\tau)$) even across heterogeneous node firing rates, enforcing $\dot{S}\_{\mathrm{prod}} = 0$ at detailed-balance equilibrium. During intact waking hierarchy, core-periphery trophic gradients break detailed balance during waking awareness and directed top-down recurrence crosses the layer-5 coincidence threshold into reportable feeling, sustaining $\dot{S}\_{\mathrm{prod}} = 0.0265 \pm 0.0004$ nats/step alongside $A_{\mathrm{asym}} = 0.0176$ (Lynn et al., 2021). Multi-scale replication across `N = 12` micro-columns, `N = 32` meso-columns, and `N = 68` human Desikan-Killiany cortical parcels (Table 3) confirms scale invariance: across all three parcellations, analytical Cheeger-Kuramoto bifurcation thresholds ($g_{\mathrm{LR}}^{\mathrm{crit}} = 0.18$) closely track empirical canary crossings (`0.16`), and intact long-range tracts preserve a `4x` to `8x` normalized Fiedler gap advantage (`0.0667` vs `0.0069` at `N = 68`).

## 6. Intrinsic Collapse Detection and Rayleigh-Fiedler Spectral Self-Rewiring
