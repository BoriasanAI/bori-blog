# On the Nature of Rhetoric

To ask what rhetoric really is turns out to be harder than it looks. The working answer, that rhetoric is the art of persuasion, carries us some way, but the structure underneath deserves a closer look.

Consider first what gives rhetoric its shape: It is one of the three ancient arts of discourse (trivium) of classical antiquity, along with grammar and logic/dialectic. This is not an incidental detail; it is the feature that lets the concept do work, marking it off from the neighbouring ideas it is so often confused.

If a single thread runs through all of this, it is that rhetoric rewards attention. The closer one looks, the more structure appears, and that, in the end, is the point.
