## The Shape of Balkans

Few ideas are invoked as often, and examined as rarely, as balkans. The usual shorthand, that balkans is The Balkans, corresponding partially with the Balkan Peninsula, is a geographical area in southeastern Europe with various geographical and historical definitions, is accurate enough, yet it leaves the more interesting question untouched: why does the concept hold together at all?



To call balkans well understood would be premature; to call it mysterious would be lazy. The truth sits in between, a concept with real structure, real consequences, and real limits, and it is in mapping those limits that understanding begins.
