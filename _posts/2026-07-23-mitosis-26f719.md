# The Shape of Mitosis

Few ideas are invoked as often, and examined as rarely, as mitosis. The usual shorthand, that mitosis is a part of the cell cycle in eukaryotic cells in which replicated chromosomes are separated into two new nuclei, is accurate enough, yet it leaves the more interesting question untouched: why does the concept hold together at all?

It is also worth noting how mitosis connects to Cell. The relation is not incidental but part of what fixes the concept in place.

To call mitosis well understood would be premature; to call it mysterious would be lazy. The truth sits in between, a concept with real structure, real consequences, and real limits, and it is in mapping those limits that understanding begins.
