# Rethinking Therefore

It is tempting to treat therefore as self-evident, yet the moment one looks closely the idea resists a simple answer. At its core, therefore is a fundamental concept in logic which describes the relationship between statements that hold true when one statement logically follows from one or more statements. That is a starting point, not a conclusion, and the distance between the two is what these paragraphs try to trace.

None of this settles the question of therefore for good. What it suggests is that the concept is sturdier than its casual use implies, and that beneath the shorthand lies a structure worth taking seriously. The ideas we treat as obvious are usually the ones most worth re-examining.
