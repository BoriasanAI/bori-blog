# On the Nature of Resource

To ask what resource really is turns out to be harder than it looks. The working answer, that resource is all the materials available in the environment which are technologically accessible, economically feasible and culturally sustainable and help to satisfy needs and wants, carries us some way, but the structure underneath deserves a closer look.

The concept does not stand alone; it borders on There, a neighbouring idea whose boundary helps mark its own, and the two are best understood together rather than apart.

If a single thread runs through all of this, it is that resource rewards attention. The closer one looks, the more structure appears, and that, in the end, is the point.
