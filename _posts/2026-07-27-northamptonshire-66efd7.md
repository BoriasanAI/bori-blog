# On the Nature of Northamptonshire

Few ideas are invoked as often, and examined as rarely, as northamptonshire. The usual shorthand, that northamptonshire is a ceremonial county in the East Midlands of England, is accurate enough, yet it leaves the more interesting question untouched: why does the concept hold together at all?

The concept does not stand alone; it borders on Warwickshire, a neighbouring idea whose boundary helps mark its own, and the two are best understood together rather than apart.

To call northamptonshire well understood would be premature; to call it mysterious would be lazy. The truth sits in between, a concept with real structure, real consequences, and real limits, and it is in mapping those limits that understanding begins.
