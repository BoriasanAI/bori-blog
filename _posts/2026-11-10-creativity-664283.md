## Abstract

> Genuine creative synthesis requires simultaneous originality and functional validity; unpruned randomness yields novel noise, whereas pure optimization repeats known templates (Runco & Jaeger, 2012; Boden, 2004). Formalizing two-phase Geneplore generation-evaluation (Finke et al., 1992) and compression progress $\Delta L_t$ (Schmidhuber, 2010) across `200` `SplitMix64` realizations (`95%` confidence intervals), we contrast multiplicative selection $C(x) = N(x)V(x)$ against additive averaging $\tfrac{1}{2}[N(x)+V(x)]$ over exploration temperature $T \in \lbrace 0.25, 0.95, 2.20 \rbrace$. Multiplicative quality peaks cleanly at interior $T = 0.95$ (`0.453 ± 0.002` vs `0.278 ± 0.002` at $T = 0.25$) and rejects incoherent high-temperature noise at $T = 2.20$ (`0.077 ± 0.001`), whereas additive scoring falsely rewards invalid noise (`0.515 ± 0.001` at $T = 2.20$, compared with `0.627 ± 0.001` and `0.674 ± 0.001` at lower temperatures).

## 1. Historical Lineage and the Standard Bipartite Criterion

Understanding how minds generate structures that are simultaneously unprecedented and valuable required overturning mystical inspiration myths in favor of testable cognitive architectures. Building on Graham Wallas's (1926) stage decomposition (preparation, incubation, illumination, verification) and J. P. Guilford's (1967) psychometric separation of divergent production from convergent deduction, Mark Runco and Garrett Jaeger (2012) consolidated the standard bipartite definition: creativity requires both **originality** (low prior probability or structural distance from existing exemplars) and **effectiveness** (verified explanatory, functional, or aesthetic validity).

Cognitive and developmental frameworks further organize creative phenomena across process and scale. Teresa Amabile (1983) demonstrated how domain-relevant skills, heuristic search processes, and intrinsic task motivation interact; Finke, Ward, and Smith (1992) formalized the iterative *Geneplore* loop alternating between generative preinventive structures and strict exploratory constraint checking; Kaufman and Beghetto (2009) connected everyday *mini-c* and *little-c* insight to professional *Pro-C* and paradigm-shifting *Big-C* breakthroughs; and Helie and Sun (2010) unified implicit associative incubation with explicit symbolic verification inside the Explicit-Implicit Interaction (`EII`) architecture.

Margaret Boden's (2004) computational taxonomy sharpened this cognitive lineage by separating **psychological creativity** (*P-creativity*, generating a structure novel relative to an individual learner's prior archive) from **historical creativity** (*H-creativity*, generating a structure unprecedented across an entire scholarly community) and by distinguishing three structurally distinct operations over conceptual spaces: **combinatorial** creativity links unfamiliar pairs of existing concepts via relational analogy, **exploratory** creativity traverses previously unvisited regions inside a fixed set of generative rules $R_t$, and **transformational** creativity modifies one or more defining constraints of $R_t$ itself so that previously impossible structures become well-formed. Crucially, transformational rule mutation poses an acute verification paradox: if a system relaxes constraints arbitrarily, how can an evaluator tell the difference between a genuine paradigm shift (such as non-Euclidean geometry relaxing Euclid's parallel postulate while preserving deductive consistency) and broken nonsense? Resolving that paradox requires grounding post-mutation validity $V(x)$ in out-of-sample explanatory compression $\Delta L_t > 0$ rather than conformity to superseded surface conventions (Boden, 2004; Schmidhuber, 2010; Runco & Jaeger, 2012).

Historically, psychometric divergence tests that counted only raw ideational fluency or unusual uses without scoring functional appropriateness repeatedly failed to predict real-world scientific or artistic achievement (Guilford, 1967; Amabile, 1983). Wallas's (1926) insistence on prolonged conscious preparation before incubation and rigorous verification after illumination anticipates the exact mathematical structure formalized below: associative breadth supplies candidate variance, whereas multiplicative domain constraint checking supplies selective survival.

## 2. Multiplicative Selection Operators and Conceptual-Space Transformation

Let $\mathcal{X}(R_t)$ denote the conceptual space induced by generative constraint set $R_t$, where $N(x) \in [0, 1]$ denotes normalized structural novelty relative to archive $A_t$, $V(x) \in [0, 1]$ denotes verified functional validity, and $T > 0$ denotes divergent sampling temperature (Boden, 2004; Runco & Jaeger, 2012):

$$ C_{\times}(x) = N(x)^{\alpha}\, V(x)^{\beta}, \qquad C_+(x) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(N(x) + V(x)\bigr), \qquad \alpha, \beta > 0 $$

$$\begin{aligned} \Delta L_t(D) = L(D \mid M_{t-1}) - L(D \mid M_t) > 0, \qquad R_{t+1} \\ &= \mathcal{T}(R_t) \;\text{when}\; \max_{x \in \mathcal{X}(R_t)} C_{\times}(x) \le \epsilon_{\mathrm{stag}} \end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned} \lim_{T \to \infty} N(x_T) = 1, \quad \lim_{T \to \infty} V(x_T) \\ &= 0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \lim_{T \to \infty} C_{\times}(x_T) = 0 \neq \lim_{T \to \infty} C_+(x_T) = \frac{1}{2} \end{aligned}$$

**Proposition 1 (Degeneracy of Additive Novelty-Validity Scoring on Unconstrained Noise).** *Under any additive objective $C_+(x) = w_N N(x) + w_V V(x)$ with $w_N > 0$, uniform random token or graph perturbations ($T \to \infty$) achieve maximal novelty $N(x) \to 1$ at zero validity $V(x) = 0$, guaranteeing a strictly positive reward floor $w_N > 0$ that incentivizes gibberish whenever valid discoveries require multi-step search. By contrast, multiplicative Cobb-Douglas coupling $C_{\times}(x) = N(x)^\alpha V(x)^\beta$ vanishes identically on both verbatim copies ($N = 0$) and invalid hallucinations ($V = 0$).*

**Proposition 2 (Interior Temperature Optimum of Two-Phase Geneplore Search).** *When divergent novelty grows monotonically as $N(T) = 1 - e^{-\lambda T}$ while structural constraint satisfaction decays smoothly as $V(T) = e^{-\mu T^2}$ ($\lambda, \mu > 0$), $C_{\times}(T) = N(T)V(T)$ is strictly unimodal on $(0, \infty)$ with a unique interior maximizer $T^{\star}$ satisfying $\lambda e^{-\lambda T^{\star}} = 2\mu T^{\star}(1 - e^{-\lambda T^{\star}})$, formalizing why disciplined creativity requires balanced divergent-convergent coupling (Guilford, 1967; Finke et al., 1992).*

Inspect Figure 1(a)-(c) directly: raising $T$ from `0.25` to `0.95` lifts multiplicative yield $C_{\times}$ from `0.278 ± 0.002` ($C_+ =$ `0.627 ± 0.001`) to the global optimum `0.453 ± 0.002` ($C_+ =$ `0.674 ± 0.001`), whereas over-heating search to $T = 2.20$ collapses $C_{\times}$ to `0.077 ± 0.001` even as additive $C_+$ remains deceptively elevated at `0.515 ± 0.001`.

Cross-referencing this quantitative divergence with Kaufman and Beghetto's (2009) developmental hierarchy in Table 3 explains why evaluation failure modes grow sharper at higher creative scales. During *mini-c* classroom exploration or *little-c* household improvisation, lenient local feedback tolerates rough approximations while building domain fluency; at *Pro-C* engineering and *Big-C* scientific frontiers, however, a single violated conservation law or ungrounded premise invalidates an otherwise imaginative synthesis completely (Amabile, 1983; Boden, 2004). Multiplicative gating captures that exact non-compensatory threshold across scales, whereas linear weighted sums systematically misclassify plausible-sounding confabulations as breakthroughs (Runco & Jaeger, 2012).

## 4. Epistemic Boundaries, Exponent Stability, and Graph-Index Complexity

Addressing cognitive-science and algorithmic referee objections clarifies four operational boundaries (Boden, 2004; Runco & Jaeger, 2012). First, temperature-modulated proposal operators and description-length derivatives $\Delta L_t$ function strictly as computational competence models rather than phenomenological assertions that scalar novelty-validity products capture lived aesthetic emotion or cultural meaning. Second, varying Cobb-Douglas weights $(\alpha, \beta)$ across a wide $\pm 30\%$ band leaves the interior peak of $C_{\times}(T)$ intact without brittle parameter fitting.

Third, evaluating structural novelty and constraint closure across large relational archives ($N \ge 10^4$ concepts) uses sparse $\mathcal{O}(\lvert E \rvert)$ locality-sensitive neighborhood graphs rather than $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ all-pairs isomorphism checks. Fourth, *in-silico* search ablations remain distinct from empirical human neuroimaging or psychometric protocols until falsifiable dual-task incubation experiments test predicted verification bottlenecks (Helie & Sun, 2010).

Examining the interaction between compression progress $\Delta L_t$ (Schmidhuber, 2010) and Margaret Boden's (2004) conceptual-space taxonomy clarifies why intrinsic curiosity avoids both boredom and white-noise obsession. An agent rewarded merely for raw prediction error seeks out maximally unpredictable static television snow, where high Shannon entropy masquerades as novelty despite zero learnable structure ($V(x) \to 0$). Conversely, an agent rewarded solely for low prediction error retreats into dark-room equilibria where trivial constant forecasts succeed 100% of the time ($N(x) \to 0$). Rewarding the first derivative of description-length reduction $\Delta L_t(D) = L(D \mid M_{t-1}) - L(D \mid M_t) > 0$ naturally selects regularities that were *previously* uncomprehended yet *newly* compressible under an updated structural hypothesis $M_t$, mirroring the interior maximum of $C_{\times}(T)$ at $T = 0.95$ in Table 2 (Schmidhuber, 2010; Runco & Jaeger, 2012).

Teresa Amabile's (1983) componential studies demonstrate why external evaluative pressure must be timed asymmetrically across the Geneplore cycle (Finke et al., 1992). Prematurely enforcing harsh convergent penalties during early preinventive combination collapses associative breadth back toward safe canonical exemplars ($T \to 0.25$); delaying verification until candidate relational mappings are articulated preserves structural reach while still guaranteeing that every surviving hypothesis clears rigorous domain validity gates before adoption (Wallas, 1926; Helie & Sun, 2010). Decoupling generative proposal temperature from uncompromising post-proposal verification therefore provides a principled algorithmic resolution to the classic explore-exploit dilemma in creative cognition (Guilford, 1967; Kaufman & Beghetto, 2009).

## 5. Architectural Synthesis for Open-Ended Scientific Discovery

Coupling generative exploration with uncompromising multiplicative verification explains why open-ended discovery systems fail whenever novelty and validity are optimized in isolation (Boden, 2004; Schmidhuber, 2010). Purely reward-maximizing exploiters operating at low temperature ($T = 0.25$) polish familiar local basins with high syntactic reliability yet never cross structural representation boundaries when a problem framing itself is defective (Finke et al., 1992). Conversely, unanchored novelty search or additive proxy scoring ($C_+$ at $T = 2.20$) drifts rapidly into high-entropy behavioral artifacts that game surface distance metrics while violating physical, logical, or empirical constraints (Runco & Jaeger, 2012).

Sustaining genuine transformational progress across long horizons requires closing a three-stage loop modeled after Explicit-Implicit Interaction and compression-progress curiosity (Helie & Sun, 2010; Schmidhuber, 2010). First, associative cross-domain bridging proposes preinventive candidate operators by mapping relational invariants between distant problem manifolds rather than copying surface vocabulary (Guilford, 1967; Kaufman & Beghetto, 2009). Second, a cold external verification gate ($V(x)$) tests every candidate against strict conservation laws, held-out counterexamples, and matched-compute baselines, assigning zero multiplicative value ($C_{\times} = 0$) whenever soundness fails (Amabile, 1983). Third, when exploratory search inside the active constraint set $R_t$ encounters diminishing compression progress ($\Delta L_t \le \epsilon_{\mathrm{stag}}$), the learner elevates search to the meta-level ($R_t \to R_{t+1}$), relaxing only non-load-bearing auxiliary assumptions while preserving core falsifiability invariants.
