# Rethinking Religious

It is tempting to treat religious as self-evident, yet the moment one looks closely the idea resists a simple answer. At its core, religious is a range of social-cultural systems, including designated behaviors and practices, ethics, morals, beliefs, worldviews, texts, sanctified places, prophecies, or organizations, that generally relate humanity to supernatural, transcendental, and spiritual elements—although there is no scho. That is a starting point, not a conclusion, and the distance between the two is what these paragraphs try to trace.

None of this settles the question of religious for good. What it suggests is that the concept is sturdier than its casual use implies, and that beneath the shorthand lies a structure worth taking seriously. The ideas we treat as obvious are usually the ones most worth re-examining.
