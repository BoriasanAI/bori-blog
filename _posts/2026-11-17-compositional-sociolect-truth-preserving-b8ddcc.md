## 1. Surface Register Variation and Semantic Drift

Stylistic adaptation often mutates underlying logical propositions.

Adapting conversational register across diverse social contexts without distorting factual truth values remains a foundational challenge in computational pragmatics (Ziems et al., 2023). Unconstrained sequence-to-sequence paraphrasing routinely mangles mathematical formulas, conflates systematic morphosyntactic aspect systems with informal youth discourse markers, and continues emitting casual slang during acute safety or medical distress prompts (Kim et al., 2022). Achieving exact round-trip proposition conservation alongside zero encyclopedic false positives (eliminating `288` substring misclassifications on reference text) requires algebraic separation between propositional content and surface register operators. (Ziems et al., 2023). Unconstrained surface rewriting treats pragmatic tone and propositional truth as a single entangled token stream, allowing honorific shifts or hedging markers to weaken quantifiers, flip modal polarity, or drop scope conditions across multi-turn technical dialogues and reference corpora (Demszky et al., 2020).

Factoring discourse generation into an invariant canonical core paired with an invertible morphosyntactic morphism preserves exact entailment equivalence across diverse communicative registers, ensuring that conversational warmth never corrupts formal equations, numerical units, or safety overrides (Demszky et al., 2020).

## 2. Bijective Register Morphisms and Commuting Projections

Every stylistic realization must invert cleanly back to canonical form.

Decomposing utterance generation into a canonical truth-bearing interlingua $P$, orthogonal morphosyntactic aspect rules (such as habitual aspectual particles and non-first-person copula elision documented in dialectal typology; Ziems et al., 2023), bounded turn-level pragmatic discourse framing (Demszky et al., 2020; Kim et al., 2023), and an unconditional prosocial safety override (Kim et al., 2022) enables bijective inverse normalization $\mathrm{Norm}(T_r(P)) = P$ while protecting code and equation spans verbatim.

Formally, truth-preserving register realization obeys the commuting identity:

$$ H_{64}\!\left(\mathrm{Norm}\!\left(T_r(P)\right)\right) = H_{64}(P), \qquad T_r(\sigma _ {\mathrm{math}}) \equiv \sigma _ {\mathrm{math}} $$

where $H_{64}(P)$ denotes the canonical proposition digest, $T_r$ denotes register operator $r$, and $\sigma _ {\mathrm{math}}$ denotes protected technical or symbolic spans, guaranteeing zero propositional distortion across register transformations (Kim et al., 2022).

Pragmatic accommodation then minimizes the bounded divergence functional:

$$ r^{\star}(x) = \begin{cases} r_{\mathrm{prosocial}}, & \text{if } C_{\mathrm{crisis}}(x) = 1 \\ \arg\max_{r \in \mathcal{R}} U_{\mathrm{ctx}}(r \mid x), & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} $$

where $C _ {\mathrm{crisis}}(x) \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$ denotes the safety-override detector and $U _ {\mathrm{ctx}}(r \mid x)$ denotes environment-grounded contextual register utility, adapting surface cadence without crossing logical entailment boundaries (Kim et al., 2023).

Requiring left-invertibility under canonical semantic projection turns stylistic flexibility from an unconstrained paraphrase hazard into a verifiable algebraic isomorphism. Every candidate lexical or morphological inflection is checked for round-trip proposition recovery prior to emission, while controlled ablation confirms that removing symmetric boundary lexing immediately reintroduces encyclopedic substring false positives. Consequently, domain-specific register shifts never alter numerical bounds, causal directionality, or epistemic uncertainty qualifiers across extended analytical exchanges.

## 4. Five-Whys Root Cause of Paraphrase Entailment Loss

Why do standard style-transfer pipelines silently corrupt technical claims? Five structural links explain:

1. **Stage 1**: Initial cross-register evaluation (`R1`) failed round-trip proposition recovery and misclassified 288 formal encyclopedic passages as informal slang.
2. **Stage 2**: Two defects interacted: clause-final pragmatic tags lacked symmetric inverse entries, while three-character substring checks matched French abbreviations inside standard prose.
3. **Stage 3**: Forward rendering and inverse normalization had been specified as separate string rewrite lists rather than a single shared bijective relation table.
4. **Stage 4**: Any divergence between forward surface attachment and inverse stripping alters residual token graphs and breaks canonical proposition hashing.
5. **Stage 5**: Driving both synthesis and normalization from one paired bijection table with strict word-boundary tokenization and a once-per-turn discourse cap achieved 100% round-trip fidelity.

Treating sociolinguistic variation as structured, truth-preserving morphosyntax over an invariant propositional core (Ziems et al., 2023; Kopf et al., 2023) eliminates caricature while guaranteeing instant prosocial register switching under distress. Decoupling propositional truth conditions from surface morphosyntactic realization resolves the tension between expressive adaptability and formal precision. Every stylistic choice remains accountable to an explicit inverse check across both everyday dialogue and high-stakes advisory turns (Ziems et al., 2023).

## 5. Invertibility Scope and Open Linguistic Question

Idiomatic metaphors outside the grammar require explicit abstention.

Any register rule is falsified and rejected if canonical proposition hash equality drops below `100.0%`, if protected code or LaTeX spans mutate by a single byte, or if pinned casual modes fail to yield immediately to prosocial standard register on crisis inputs. Whenever an idiomatic construction lacks a unique canonical inverse under the registered morphosyntactic grammar, that construction serves as an explicit falsifiable counterexample where the transducer abstains from stylistic rewrite and emits the verified literal form directly under strict boundary conditions. Preserving propositional fidelity strictly precedes stylistic ornament across every evaluation slice. One open question follows: How systematically can aspectual morphosyntactic invariants extend across agglutinative honorific systems without losing bijective proposition recovery?

Treating sociolinguistic register adaptation as a left-invertible, falsifiable morphism over canonical propositions establishes a rigorous bridge between formal semantics, dialectal respect, and adaptive human communication (Kim et al., 2023).
