## On the Nature of Greeting

To ask what greeting really is turns out to be harder than it looks. The working answer, that greeting is an act of communication in which human beings intentionally make their presence known to each other, to show attention, and to suggest a type of relationship or social status between individuals or groups of people coming in contact with each other, carries us some way, but the structure underneath deserves a closer look.



If a single thread runs through all of this, it is that greeting rewards attention. The closer one looks, the more structure appears, and that, in the end, is the point.
