## Abstract

> Wait past minus one-half and actuator limits guarantee impact. Composing rate-distortion trade-offs on predictive variables (Information Theory) with optic-flow invariants and time-to-contact (Embodied Cognition) replaces qualitative analogy with falsifiable numerical margins.
{: .prompt-tip }

## 1. Breakdown Regimes Across Uncoupled Information Theory and Embodied Cognition

Counterexamples across both axes constrain any valid cross-domain law. Compressing for a single narrow task can discard structural features that become decisive once goals shift, creating brittle representations blind to novel causal affordances (Tishby et al., 2000). Compression is not merely a storage convenience; discarding nuisance variation while retaining predictive sufficiency is what turns raw measurements into transferable concepts (Tishby et al., 2000). Modeling Information Theory without Embodied Cognition leaves critical instability thresholds invisible in single-domain sweeps.

Recording every microscopic jitter verbatim leaves a learner trapped in high-dimensional noise where no two experiences ever look alike enough to support generalization (Alemi et al., 2017). Thermodynamics summarizes astronomical molecular velocities into pressure, volume, and temperature because microscopic phase coordinates add zero macroscopic predictive leverage at equilibrium (Tishby et al., 2000; Gibson, 1979). Sufficient statistics in mathematical inference formalize the identical insight: a minimal summary preserves every bit of parameter Fisher information while stripping sample noise (Gibson, 1979).

## 2. Joint Operator Structure: Rate-Distortion Trade-Offs on Predictive Variables and Optic-Flow Invariants and Time-to-Contact

Tishby's Information Bottleneck trades off mutual information between inputs and latent codes against mutual information between latent codes and downstream target variables (Alemi et al., 2017). In Information Theory, where $X$ denotes raw high-dimensional observation, $Z$ denotes compressed bottleneck code under stochastic encoder $q(z\mid x)$, $Y$ denotes target variable, and $\beta > 0$ denotes the relevance Lagrange multiplier (Alemi et al., 2017).

$$ $$ \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{IB}}[p(z \mid x)] = I(X; Z) - \beta\, I(Z; Y) $$ $$

Meanwhile, Embodied Cognition (*Optic-Flow Invariants and Time-to-Contact*) enforces the partner balance (Gibson, 1979):

$$ $$ \theta(t) = 2\arctan\!\left(\frac{R}{z(t)}\right) \underset{R/z \ll 1}{\approx} \frac{2R}{z(t)}, \qquad \tau(t) = \frac{\sin\theta(t)}{\dot{\theta}(t)} = \frac{z(t)}{-\dot{z}(t)} $$ $$

#### Table 1. Variational Information Bottleneck Quantities, Fixed-Point Laws, and Plane Bounds

| Symbol | Information Quantity | Analytical Expression | Operating Scale | Compression Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $L_{\mathrm{IB}}$ | Bottleneck Lagrangian | $I(X; Z) - \beta\, I(Z; Y)$ | Nat / bit functional | Trades code rate against predictive value |
| $I(X; Z)$ | Representational rate | $\mathbb{E}[D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q(z \mid x) \parallel q(z))]$ | $\ge 0$ bits | Penalizes memorized sensor nuisance |
| $I(Z; Y)$ | Predictive sufficiency | $I(X; Y) - \mathbb{E}[D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p(y\mid x)\parallel q(y\mid z))]$ | $\le I(X; Y)$ bits | Measures retained task-relevant structure |
| $\beta$ | Relevance multiplier | Inverse temperature slope $dI(X;Z)/dI(Z;Y)$ | $\beta \in (0, \infty)$ | Sweeps coarse-to-fine representation grain |
| $\beta_c^{(k)}$ | Bifurcation threshold | $(1 - \sigma_k^2)^{-1}$ of $p(y \mid x)$ | Discrete critical knees | Triggers cluster splitting on info plane |

Source: [Tishby et al. (2000)](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0004057); [Alemi et al. (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00410)

*Note.* Data Processing Inequality enforces $I(Z; Y) \le \min(I(X; Z), I(X; Y))$ across every stochastic encoder $q(z \mid x)$.


Formalizing the interface between *Rate-Distortion Trade-Offs on Predictive Variables* (Information Theory; Tishby et al., 2000) and *Optic-Flow Invariants and Time-to-Contact* (Embodied Cognition; Gibson, 1979) embeds Normalized Compression Penalty 1 / beta observables inside Normalized Approach Horizon Toward Obstacle coarse projection $b = e^{\eta}$:

$$ J_{AB}(\eta) = DF_A \circ \Pi_{AB}(e^{\eta}), \qquad \mathcal{E}(\eta) = \sigma_A^2\, e^{-2 \gamma_1 \eta} + \beta_{AB}\, (e^{\eta} - 1)^4 $$

Selective attenuation of nuisance channels (Lee, 1976) sharpens closed-loop tracking until quartic distortion dominates at large scale, fixing the optimal coupling at $\hat{\eta}\_{AB}^{\star} = 0.05$ ($b^{\star} \approx 1.05$).
