# Sandcastle: Structure and Consequence

Few ideas are invoked as often, and examined as rarely, as sandcastle. The usual shorthand, that sandcastle is the practice of modelling sand into an artistic form, is accurate enough, yet it leaves the more interesting question untouched: why does the concept hold together at all?

Part of what makes sandcastle resist a tidy definition is that it is assembled from several strands at once — sand brushing, sand sculpting, sand painting, and creating sand bottles — and it is their combination, rather than any single one, that does the defining work.

To call sandcastle well understood would be premature; to call it mysterious would be lazy. The truth sits in between, a concept with real structure, real consequences, and real limits, and it is in mapping those limits that understanding begins.
