## Findings

It is worth arguing that the authors propose Orchestration Reward Modeling (OrchRM), a self-supervised framework for evaluating orchestration quality without human annotations.

<blockquote><p>OrchRM leverages intermediate artifacts from multi-agent executions to construct win-lose pairs for Bradley-Terry reward model training.</p><cite><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13598" rel="noopener noreferrer">[2606.13598] Reward Modeling for Multi-Agent Orchestration</a> · 2026-11-01</cite></blockquote>

Notably, These gains consistently transfer across multiple domains, including mathematical reasoning, web-based question answering, and multi-hop reasoning, demonstrating orchestration-level reward modeling as a scalable direction for robust multi-agent orchestration.

<blockquote><p>OrchRM improves training efficiency by up to 10x in token usage while improving MAS test-time scaling performance by up to 8% in accuracy.</p><cite><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13598" rel="noopener noreferrer">[2606.13598] Reward Modeling for Multi-Agent Orchestration</a> · 2026-11-01</cite></blockquote>

## Recommendation

Taken together, these points suggest that Reward Modeling for Multi-Agent Orchestration deserves more deliberate attention than it usually receives.
