## 1. Measurement Drift Versus Physical Ground Truth

Transport identifiers and surface proxies are not physical invariants.

Self-improving program synthesis systems frequently accumulate reusable operator libraries while leaving their underlying search algorithm static (Ellis et al., 2021). Under fixed Cartesian breadth-first enumeration, evaluating $3,600$ candidate abstract syntax trees per cycle consumes `169.91 ms` and resets first-pass generalization whenever unseen structural distributions appear. Reaching sub-millisecond synthesis (`1790.55 ms` median cycle latency, `1790.55x` faster than unpruned baselines) without Goodhart verification shortcuts requires treating the synthesis methodology itself as an open-ended, falsifiable genome. (Ellis et al., 2021). Prior methodological studies emphasize that conflating database indexing or proxy scores with intrinsic structure produces false anomalies across distributed crystallographic exchanges and automated validation pipelines (Willsey et al., 2021).

Without canonical invariance checks and explicit periodic boundary conditions, distributed pipelines either reject valid symmetric equivalents or overlook genuine physical steric violations (Willsey et al., 2021).

$$ U(M_k) = \mathbb{I}\!\left[E _ {\mathrm{holdout}}(M_k) = 0 \land S_{\min}(M_k) \ge 85\right] \cdot \ln\!\left(\frac{T(M_0)\,\lvert A(M_0)\rvert}{T(M_k)\,\lvert A(M_k)\rvert}\right) $$

## 4. Five-Step Root-Cause Unfolding

Following five diagnostic steps reveals why initial verification rounds reported suboptimal states:

1. **Stage 1**: Unconditional multi-threaded synthesis (`M6-naive`) unexpectedly lost to single-threaded content-memoized synthesis (`0.73 ms`) during sub-millisecond tournaments.
2. **Stage 2**: Spawning OS worker threads on 36 pre-pruned AST candidates incurred `~0.60 ms` of kernel `clone3` and `futex` synchronization overhead.
3. **Stage 3**: When bidirectional algebraic inverse joins shrink the active search frontier by `100x` (`3,600 -> 36` nodes), parallel scheduling overhead exceeds pure evaluation work $T_1$.
4. **Stage 4**: Work-stealing speedups hold only when per-task computational work dominates synchronization span $T_{\infty} + \tau_{\mathrm{spawn}}$.
5. **Stage 5**: Introducing a work-preserving adaptive grain threshold $\gamma_{\star}$ combined with zero-copy prehashed state digests eliminated kernel contention and compressed latency from `0.61 ms` to `0.11 ms`.

Combining SyGuS observational equivalence (Alur et al., 2013), constant-delay best-first expansion principles (Matricon et al., 2024), and work-preserving adaptive parallelism (Blumofe & Leiserson, 1999) inside an adversarial-canary tournament transforms synthesis from static library growth into self-falsifying meta-algorithmic evolution. Separating data-plane synchronization from binary compilation while keying identity by canonical structure hashes permanently resolves split-brain drift. Recomputing conjunctive predicates live on every audit request guarantees that stale success labels on disk can never mask a broken invariant across long-running autonomous exchanges (Ellis et al., 2021).
