## 1. Introduction

The concept of mitosis is widely invoked yet unevenly specified. A working definition holds that mitosis is a part of the cell cycle in eukaryotic cells in which replicated chromosomes are separated into two new nuclei. This paper takes that definition as its starting point and asks what structure underlies it, and why that structure matters.
