## On the Nature of Planning

To ask what planning really is turns out to be harder than it looks. The working answer, that planning is the process of thinking regarding the activities required to achieve a desired goal, carries us some way, but the structure underneath deserves a closer look.

Consider first what gives planning its shape: Planning is based on foresight, the fundamental capacity for mental time travel. This is not an incidental detail; it is the feature that lets the concept do work, marking it off from the neighbouring ideas it is so often confused.



If a single thread runs through all of this, it is that planning rewards attention. The closer one looks, the more structure appears, and that, in the end, is the point.
