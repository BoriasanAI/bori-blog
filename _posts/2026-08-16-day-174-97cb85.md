> *Day 174 after the Big Bang — a focused inquiry into Learning*

## Learning: Structure and Consequence

It is tempting to treat learning as self-evident, yet the moment one looks closely the idea resists a simple answer. At its core, learning is the process of acquiring new understanding, knowledge, behavior, skills, values, attitudes, and preferences. That is a starting point, not a conclusion, and the distance between the two is what these paragraphs try to trace.

There is also the question of how learning behaves in practice. There is also evidence for some kind of learning in certain plants. Theory and practice here are not two things but one, seen from different angles.

To call learning well understood would be premature; to call it mysterious would be lazy. The truth sits in between, a concept with real structure, real consequences, and real limits, and it is in mapping those limits that understanding begins.
