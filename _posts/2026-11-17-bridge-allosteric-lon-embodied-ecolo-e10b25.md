## 1. Why Isolated Biophysics Fails Under Embodied Cognition Perturbations

Counterexamples across both axes constrain any valid cross-domain law. Rigid lock-and-key models miss entropic allostery altogether, where signaling travels through altered vibrational fluctuations even while mean backbone coordinates barely move (Monod et al., 1965). Proteins act as physical logic gates when ligand binding at a distal regulatory pocket reshapes catalytic affinity nanometers away without direct steric contact (Monod et al., 1965). Modeling Biophysics without Embodied Cognition leaves critical instability thresholds invisible in single-domain sweeps.

Living cells process thousands of concurrent chemical signals inside crowded cytoplasm by encoding conditional switches directly into macromolecular folding landscapes (Changeux & Edelstein, 2005). Hemoglobin's oxygen binding curve turns sigmoidal because binding a single oxygen molecule strains heme iron geometry across subunit interfaces, priming neighboring sites for uptake (Monod et al., 1965; Gibson, 1979). Mechanical metamaterials and origami tensegrity lattices exhibit the same distal actuation when localized hinge buckling propagates concerted strain modes across an entire truss (Gibson, 1979).

## 2. Joint Operator Structure: Population Shifts Across Quaternary Ensembles and Optic-Flow Invariants and Time-to-Contact

Monod, Wyman, and Changeux showed that thermal conformational ensembles shift their free-energy minimum when a modulator stabilizes one cooperative quaternary state over another (Changeux & Edelstein, 2005). In Biophysics, where $\alpha = [X] / K_R$ denotes normalized ligand concentration relative to relaxed-state dissociation constant $K_R$, $L = [T_0]/[R_0] \gg 1$ denotes the unliganded conformational equilibrium ratio, $c = K_R / K_T \ll 1$ denotes state affinity asymmetry, and $n$ denotes oligomeric subunit count (Changeux & Edelstein, 2005).

$$ $$ \bar{R}([X]) = \frac{(1 + [X] / K_R)^n}{(1 + [X] / K_R)^n + L_0\, (1 + c\, [X] / K_R)^n} $$ $$

Meanwhile, Embodied Cognition (*Optic-Flow Invariants and Time-to-Contact*) enforces the partner balance (Gibson, 1979):

$$ $$ \theta(t) = 2\arctan\!\left(\frac{R}{z(t)}\right) \underset{R/z \ll 1}{\approx} \frac{2R}{z(t)}, \qquad \tau(t) = \frac{\sin\theta(t)}{\dot{\theta}(t)} = \frac{z(t)}{-\dot{z}(t)} $$ $$
