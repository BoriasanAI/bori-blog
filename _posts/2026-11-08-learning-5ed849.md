## Abstract

> Adaptive learning succeeds neither by memorizing every coincidence nor by freezing fixed instincts, but by matching eligibility credit windows and plasticity rates to environmental causal structure. Across `200` `SplitMix64` trace-conditioning runs, matched eligibility decay $\tau_e = 0.50\;\mathrm{s}$ achieves `0.807 ± 0.002` credit signal-to-noise while suppressing spurious background drift to `0.097 ± 0.001`.
{: .prompt-tip }

## 1. Beyond Passive Accumulation: Filtering Noise From Contingency

Everyday speech treats learning as piling up isolated facts, yet biological and computational learners face a sharper selective problem: separating genuine causal contingencies from coincidental background chatter (Rescorla & Wagner, 1972; Sutton & Barto, 1998). Even the simplest non-associative adjustments obey strict energetic economy. Through synaptic depression in *Aplysia* gill-withdrawal reflexes, Eric Kandel (2001) showed that **habituation** attenuates costly responses to harmless repeated stimuli while preserving immediate responsiveness to novel threats, whereas **sensitization** recruits serotonergic interneurons to amplify defensive gain after noxious shocks.

Moving from single-stimulus calibration to relational conditioning introduces a fundamental temporal gap: causal actions precede their environmental consequences across variable delays, while unrelated background cues fire continuously in between (Pavlov, 1927). Simulating `200`-seed delayed-reinforcement episodes ($\Delta t_{\mathrm{delay}} = 0.45 \pm 0.05\;\mathrm{s}$) across eligibility time constant $\tau_e \in \lbrace 0.15, 0.50, 1.80 \rbrace\;\mathrm{s}$ exposes this temporal credit trade-off directly: overly narrow $\tau_e = 0.15\;\mathrm{s}$ decays before the outcome arrives (`0.589 ± 0.008` credit precision, `0.034 ± 0.001` spurious load), matched $\tau_e = 0.50\;\mathrm{s}$ lifts credit precision to `0.807 ± 0.002` (`0.097 ± 0.001` spurious load), and overly broad $\tau_e = 1.80\;\mathrm{s}$ inflates superstitious background capture to `0.332 ± 0.001` (`0.701 ± 0.001` precision).

## 2. Prediction-Error Blocking and Volatility-Matched Gain

Write the governing synaptic and policy update in closed form, where $\delta_t$ denotes reward prediction error, $e_i(t)$ denotes the decaying synaptic eligibility trace of cue $x_i(t)$, and $\eta_t$ denotes volatility-scaled learning rate (Rescorla & Wagner, 1972; Behrens et al., 2007):

$$ \frac{dw_i}{dt} = \eta_t\, \delta_t\, e_i(t), \qquad \tau_e \frac{de_i}{dt} = -e_i(t) + x_i(t), \qquad \delta_t = r_t + \gamma V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t) $$

$$ \eta_t^{\star} = \frac{\Sigma_{t \mid t-1}}{\Sigma_{t \mid t-1} + \sigma_{\mathrm{obs}}^2} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \lim_{v_{\mathrm{env}} \to 0} \eta_t^{\star} = 0, \quad \lim_{v_{\mathrm{env}} \gg \sigma_{\mathrm{obs}}^2} \eta_t^{\star} \to 1 $$

Temporal contiguity alone never suffices for associative learning: Kamin's blocking effect and Rescorla and Wagner's (1972) error-driven rule proved that when cue $A$ already predicts an outcome ($\delta_t = 0$), pairing a redundant compound $AB$ induces zero new weight change on $B$ despite repeated co-occurrence (Pavlov, 1927; Schultz et al., 1997). Midbrain dopaminergic phasic firing encodes this exact temporal-difference residual $\delta_t$, gating corticostriatal plasticity exclusively when expectations are violated while leaving consolidated predictions undisturbed.

Notice why this prediction-error gate solves an otherwise fatal interference problem in dense sensory streams. Every real-world episode presents hundreds of incidental room acoustics, lighting fluctuations, and postural sensations alongside the true causal predictor. A pure Hebbian correlator that strengthened every active synapse whenever reward arrived would rapidly saturate synaptic weights with accidental correlations; scaling updates by $\delta_t$ ensures that once reliable predictors explain expected return, redundant bystanders stop acquiring spurious associative weight (Rescorla & Wagner, 1972; Sutton & Barto, 1998).

## 3. Biological and Algorithmic Benchmarks Across Volatility Regimes

Evolutionary and Bayesian analyses explain why plasticity itself must be regulated by environmental non-stationarity (Behrens et al., 2007). In a frozen world ($v_{\mathrm{env}} = 0$), fixed innate wiring avoids exploration risk and metabolic overhead; in a memoryless white-noise world, yesterday's observation carries zero predictive mutual information about tomorrow. Learning earns positive net survival value strictly in environments with intermediate autocorrelation, where anterior cingulate volatility tracking raises $\eta_t^{\star}$ after structural regime shifts and damps $\eta_t^{\star}$ during stable plateaus (Behrens et al., 2007). Observational and social learning extend this efficiency further: as Albert Bandura (1977) demonstrated, attending to a skilled model's action-outcome contingencies compresses hazardous trial-and-error exploration into vicarious forward-model updates without requiring direct punishment. Inspect Figure 1(a)-(c) directly: matched eligibility and volatility-calibrated gain preserve credit fidelity across abrupt contingency reversals where fixed-gain learners either lag or oscillate.

Cellular consolidation mechanisms mirror this multi-timescale division between rapid acquisition and durable retention (Kandel, 2001). Transient short-term sensitization modifies existing synaptic proteins reversibly over minutes via cyclic-AMP second-messenger cascades, testing whether an environmental perturbation recurs before committing scarce transcriptional resources. Only repeated spaced prediction errors recruit CREB-mediated gene expression and structural spine growth, converting labile eligibility traces into long-term memory without overwriting long-standing skills after a single noisy outlier (Kandel, 2001; Schultz et al., 1997).

#### Table 2. Learning Architectures, Governing Signals, and Boundary Breakdowns

| Learning Level | Primary Driving Signal | Biological / Algorithmic Realization | Falsifiable Breakdown Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Non-associative | Stimulus repetition / shock | Presynaptic depression & serotonergic facilitation (Kandel, 2001) | Dishabituates or saturates under novel context |
| Error-driven conditioning | Prediction residual $\delta_t$ | Phasic dopamine & Rescorla-Wagner blocking (Schultz et al., 1997) | Blocked when prior cue explains 100% of outcome |
| Temporal-difference trace | Eligibility $e_i(t)\,\delta_t$ | Synaptic tag-and-capture & TD($\lambda$; Sutton & Barto, 1998) | Fails if outcome delay exceeds $\tau_e$ horizon |
| Meta-volatility adaptation | Change-point variance $\Sigma_t$ | Anterior cingulate gain modulation (Behrens et al., 2007) | Overfits observation noise if $\eta_t$ is clamped high |

Source: [Kandel (2001)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1067020); [Behrens et al. (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn1954).

## 4. Balancing Stability and Plasticity in Continual Agents

Continual autonomous agents confront the stability-plasticity dilemma across non-stationary task sequences: aggressive uniform gradient steps adapt quickly to fresh contingencies yet catastrophically erase rare safety invariants learned earlier, whereas frozen weights fail whenever physical plant dynamics drift (Sutton & Barto, 1998; Behrens et al., 2007). Combining bounded eligibility windows $\tau_e$, surprise-gated temporal-difference updates $\delta_t$, and hierarchical volatility estimation $\Sigma_{t \mid t-1}$ isolates weight changes to the specific causal pathways responsible for unexpected residuals while shielding unrelated competencies from drift (Rescorla & Wagner, 1972; Bandura, 1977).

## 5. Falsifiable Ablations and Reproducibility Contract

Two falsifiable ablations separate causal learning from raw co-occurrence counting: (1) pre-train cue $A \to R$ to asymptote before presenting compound $AB \to R$, checking that $\Delta w_B \approx 0$ rather than growing linearly with pairing count (Rescorla & Wagner, 1972), and (2) alternate stable high-noise blocks with low-noise reversal blocks to verify whether $\eta_t$ tracks true change-point volatility rather than raw observation variance (Behrens et al., 2007). Every `200`-seed trajectory (`1.96` standard-error `95% CI`) is reproducible from the <a href="https://boriasanai.github.io/bori-blog/assets/sim/learning-5ed849.rs">standalone simulation source</a> (`FNV1a-64 = 0xf73e7ed831353dc1`). How should a lifelong learner distinguish irreversible environmental drift requiring weight overwriting from temporary context switches requiring latent state indexing?
