# The Shape of Multiple choice

It is tempting to treat multiple choice as self-evident, yet the moment one looks closely the idea resists a simple answer. At its core, multiple choice is Multiple choice, multiple choice question, or objective response is a form of an objective assessment in which respondents are asked to select only the correct answer from the choices offered as a list. That is a starting point, not a conclusion, and the distance between the two is what these paragraphs try to trace.

It is also worth noting how multiple choice connects to Multiple. The relation is not incidental but part of what fixes the concept in place.

None of this settles the question of multiple choice for good. What it suggests is that the concept is sturdier than its casual use implies, and that beneath the shorthand lies a structure worth taking seriously. The ideas we treat as obvious are usually the ones most worth re-examining.
