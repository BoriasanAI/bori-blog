## Rethinking Questions

A question is an utterance which serves as a request for information.

<blockquote><p>Rhetorical questions , for instance, are interrogative in form but may not be considered bona fide questions, as they are not expected to be answered.</p><cite><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Question" rel="noopener noreferrer">Question - Wikipedia</a> · 2026-11-01</cite></blockquote>



None of this settles the question of questions for good. What it suggests is that the concept is sturdier than its casual use implies, and that beneath the shorthand lies a structure worth taking seriously. The ideas we treat as obvious are usually the ones most worth re-examining.
