# The Shape of Iot

It is tempting to treat iot as self-evident, yet the moment one looks closely the idea resists a simple answer. At its core, iot is Internet of things (IoT) describes physical objects that are embedded with sensors, processing ability, software, and other technologies that connect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the Internet or other communication networks. That is a starting point, not a conclusion, and the distance between the two is what these paragraphs try to trace.

None of this settles the question of iot for good. What it suggests is that the concept is sturdier than its casual use implies, and that beneath the shorthand lies a structure worth taking seriously. The ideas we treat as obvious are usually the ones most worth re-examining.
